# Community Guidelines

## CyberRecon Suite Community Standards

Welcome to the CyberRecon Suite community! These guidelines help ensure our community remains professional, helpful, and focused on advancing cybersecurity knowledge and practice.

### Our Mission

We're building a community of cybersecurity professionals dedicated to:
- **Advancing Security**: Improving cybersecurity practices and knowledge
- **Ethical Practice**: Promoting responsible and ethical security testing
- **Knowledge Sharing**: Helping each other learn and grow
- **Innovation**: Pushing the boundaries of cybersecurity technology

### Community Values

#### 🛡️ Security First
- Prioritize security and ethical practices in all discussions
- Share knowledge responsibly without exposing vulnerabilities
- Promote authorized testing and legal compliance
- Report security issues through proper channels

#### 🤝 Professional Respect
- Treat all community members with courtesy and respect
- Maintain professional communication standards
- Value diverse perspectives and experience levels
- Support newcomers and encourage learning

#### 📚 Knowledge Sharing
- Share experiences, tips, and best practices generously
- Provide constructive feedback and assistance
- Document solutions for common problems
- Contribute to community resources and documentation

#### 🔍 Technical Excellence
- Strive for accuracy in technical discussions
- Back up claims with evidence and references
- Acknowledge when you don't know something
- Continuously learn and improve your skills

### Expected Behavior

#### Positive Contributions
- **Ask Good Questions**: Research first, then ask specific, clear questions
- **Provide Helpful Answers**: Share knowledge and experience generously  
- **Share Resources**: Link to useful tools, articles, and documentation
- **Give Credit**: Acknowledge others' contributions and sources
- **Stay On-Topic**: Keep discussions relevant to cybersecurity and CyberRecon Suite

#### Professional Communication
- Use clear, professional language
- Be patient with those learning
- Provide constructive criticism, not personal attacks
- Respect confidentiality and sensitive information
- Avoid self-promotion and spam

### Prohibited Behavior

#### Security Violations
- **❌ Unauthorized Testing**: Never share techniques for unauthorized access
- **❌ Malicious Content**: No malware, exploits, or harmful code
- **❌ Illegal Activities**: No discussion of illegal hacking or criminal activities
- **❌ Data Breaches**: Never share compromised data or credentials
- **❌ Social Engineering**: No manipulation or deception of community members

#### Community Violations  
- **❌ Harassment**: No bullying, threats, or personal attacks
- **❌ Discrimination**: No discrimination based on identity or background
- **❌ Spam**: No excessive self-promotion or off-topic content
- **❌ Misinformation**: No deliberate spread of false information
- **❌ Privacy Violations**: Respect others' privacy and confidentiality

### Community Channels

#### GitHub Discussions
- **General Discussion**: Broad topics and community chat
- **Q&A**: Technical questions and troubleshooting help
- **Ideas**: Feature requests and enhancement suggestions
- **Show and Tell**: Share projects and achievements

#### GitHub Issues
- **Bug Reports**: Software bugs and technical problems only
- **Feature Requests**: Specific feature suggestions with use cases
- **Documentation**: Issues with documentation and guides

#### Professional Networks
- **LinkedIn**: Professional networking and industry news
- **Twitter**: Quick updates and community highlights
- **Conferences**: In-person networking and knowledge sharing

### Moderation and Enforcement

#### Community Moderators
Our moderation team includes:
- CyberRecon Suite maintainers and developers
- Experienced community members and security professionals
- Subject matter experts in various cybersecurity domains

#### Enforcement Actions
Violations may result in:
1. **Warning**: Private message about guideline violations
2. **Temporary Suspension**: Short-term removal from community platforms
3. **Permanent Ban**: Complete removal for serious or repeated violations
4. **Legal Action**: For illegal activities or serious security violations

#### Appeals Process
If you believe enforcement action was unfair:
1. Contact community moderators via email
2. Provide specific details about the situation
3. Include any relevant context or evidence
4. Appeals will be reviewed by senior moderators

### Reporting Issues

#### How to Report
- **Security Issues**: security@bulletdropstudios.com (private)
- **Community Violations**: community@bulletdropstudios.com
- **General Feedback**: Use GitHub Discussions
- **Urgent Issues**: Use priority support channels for subscribers

#### What to Include
- Specific details about the issue or violation
- Links to relevant posts or conversations
- Screenshots or evidence (if applicable)
- Your suggested resolution

### Global Considerations

#### International Community
Our community includes members from around the world:
- Respect different legal frameworks and regulations
- Be aware of time zone differences
- Use inclusive language that translates well
