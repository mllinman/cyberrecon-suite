# Privacy Policy

## CyberRecon Suite Privacy Policy

*Effective Date: January 2024*

### Our Commitment to Privacy

BulletDrop Studios ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of CyberRecon Suite users ("you" or "your"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our cybersecurity software.

### Information We Collect

#### Automatically Collected Information
- **Usage Statistics**: Feature usage, performance metrics, error reports
- **System Information**: Operating system, hardware specifications, software version
- **Technical Data**: Log files, crash reports, debug information (when enabled)

#### Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Username, email address, subscription details
- **Profile Data**: Optional profile information, preferences, settings
- **Support Communications**: Messages, feedback, and support requests

#### Security Data
- **Event Data**: Security events processed through SIEM functionality
- **Monitoring Data**: Network monitoring results (stored locally only)
- **Case Information**: Incident response cases and investigation data

### How We Use Information

#### Service Operation
- Provide and maintain CyberRecon Suite functionality
- Process security events and generate alerts
- Manage user accounts and subscriptions
- Deliver customer support and assistance

#### Product Improvement
- Analyze usage patterns to improve software features
- Identify and fix bugs and performance issues
- Develop new capabilities and enhancements
- Conduct security research (with anonymized data only)

#### Communication
- Send important service updates and security notices
- Respond to support requests and inquiries
- Provide subscription and billing information
- Share relevant cybersecurity threat intelligence

### Information Sharing and Disclosure

#### We Do NOT Share
- Personal security monitoring data
- Specific vulnerability information
- Proprietary security configurations
- Sensitive investigation details

#### We MAY Share
- **Aggregated Statistics**: Anonymous usage statistics for research
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or court order
- **Safety**: To prevent harm or illegal activity
- **Business Transfer**: In case of merger or acquisition (with notice)

### Data Security and Protection

#### Technical Safeguards
- **Encryption**: All sensitive data encrypted using AES-256
- **Secure Transmission**: TLS 1.3 for all network communications
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access with principle of least privilege
- **Regular Audits**: Periodic security assessments and penetration testing

#### Operational Safeguards
- **Employee Training**: Regular security awareness training
- **Incident Response**: Comprehensive breach response procedures
- **Data Minimization**: Collect only necessary information
- **Retention Limits**: Automatic data deletion after specified periods

### Your Rights and Choices

#### Account Management
- Access and update your profile information
- Change privacy and notification preferences
- Download your data in portable formats
- Delete your account and associated data

#### Marketing Communications
- Opt-out of promotional emails at any time
- Manage communication preferences in account settings
- Unsubscribe links in all marketing communications

#### Data Rights (where applicable)
- **Right to Access**: Request copies of your personal information
- **Right to Rectification**: Correct inaccurate information
- **Right to Erasure**: Request deletion of your data
- **Right to Portability**: Receive data in machine-readable format

### Cookies and Tracking

#### Local Storage
- **Application Settings**: Preferences and configuration data
- **Session Data**: Authentication tokens and temporary data
- **Performance Data**: Local caching for improved performance

#### Analytics
- **Usage Analytics**: Anonymous feature usage statistics
- **Performance Monitoring**: Application performance metrics
- **Error Tracking**: Crash reports and error logs (when enabled)

### Children's Privacy

CyberRecon Suite is intended for professional cybersecurity use and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

### International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers.

### Retention Periods

#### Account Data
- **Active Accounts**: Retained while account is active
- **Inactive Accounts**: Deleted after 2 years of inactivity
- **Deleted Accounts**: Permanently deleted within 30 days

#### Technical Data
- **Usage Statistics**: Retained for 2 years maximum
- **Error Logs**: Deleted after 90 days
- **Support Records**: Retained for 3 years

#### Security Data
- **Event Logs**: Retention based on subscription plan
- **Monitoring Data**: Stored locally on user systems only
- **Investigation Data**: Deleted when cases are closed

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of significant changes via:
- Email notification to registered users
- Prominent notice in the application
- Updates to this document with revision date

### Regional Privacy Laws

#### European Union (GDPR)
If you are in the EU, you have additional rights under GDPR including data portability, right to be forgotten, and right to object to processing.

#### California (CCPA)
California residents have rights to know about, delete, and opt-out of the sale of personal information.
